# Tracking and portfolio

EBIOS RM does not stop when Workshop 5 is validated: risk **lives on**. The tool
offers three views to steer it over time.

## Portfolio view

**Portfolio** menu. Consolidates **all your studies** into one table:

- residual exposure (number of high residual risks not accepted);
- treatment-plan progress (measures done / total);
- overdue measures (deadline passed);
- NIS2 coverage.

The table is sorted by decreasing exposure. The export button produces an
**Excel workbook** of the portfolio.

## N / N-1 change tracking

On a study's **Tracking** page (link from the dashboard), the **evolution**
section compares the **last two validated versions of Workshop 5**:

- per risk scenario, the **trend** of the residual risk (improvement, stable,
  degradation, new);
- the plan's evolution (measures added, removed, done).

For the comparison to read well, give a **campaign label** to each Workshop 5
validation ("2026 annual review").

## Tracking indicators (KRI)

Still on the **Tracking** page:

- **Automatic indicators**: computed live (plan progress, high risks not
  accepted, share of scenarios at reduced risk, NIS2 coverage...).
- **Manual indicators**: create your own KRIs (name, category, unit, target,
  alert threshold, improvement direction). Add **dated points**; a **sparkline**
  plots the series and the value turns to alert when the latest point crosses
  the threshold.

## Annual re-assessment

The recommended approach:

1. Reopen Workshop 5 (or the workshop affected by a change).
2. Update likelihoods, measures, statuses and deadlines.
3. Re-validate with a new campaign label.
4. Check the evolution section to objectivise what improved or degraded.

## Tips

- An **accepted risk** is not a closed risk: it must reappear at each review.
- A few well-tracked indicators are worth more than many abandoned ones.
