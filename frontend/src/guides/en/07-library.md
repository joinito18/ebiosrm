# Library

The **library** gathers reusable items from one study to the next, in seven
categories: **measures**, **risk origins**, **stakeholders**, **business
values**, **supporting assets**, **feared events** and **operating modes**.

## Two origins

- **System catalogue**: provided out of the box, identical on every
  installation, not editable. It contains in particular ISO/IEC 27002:2022
  (93 measures), the ANSSI hygiene guide (42 measures), and typical RO/TO
  pairs, stakeholders, business values, supporting assets, feared events and
  operating modes inspired by the method's examples.
- **Personal library**: your own items, visible only to you, until community
  publication.

## Using the library

### Library page

**Library** menu. The **My library / Community** toggle at the top changes the
scope; the tabs pick the category.

In *My library* mode, each tab lets you **add** a personal item and **remove**
one. System entries cannot be deleted.

### From a workshop

In the add forms of Workshops 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5, the **From the library** link
opens a picker: choosing an entry **pre-fills** the form fields. You then
adjust to the study's context and validate normally.

Indicative levels (a feared event's severity, a stakeholder's dependence /
penetration, an operating mode's ratings...) are **starting points**: they must
always be confirmed in your context.

### Capitalise from a study

The **→ library** button on some items (RO/TO pairs, treatment measures) adds
the current item to your personal library.

### Contextual suggestions

In Workshop 5, a **library suggestions** panel proposes measures that match the
study's content (feared events, supporting assets, RO/TO pairs, attack paths),
showing the keywords that surfaced each one. **Use** opens the add form
pre-filled.

## Community library

The **Community** toggle shows items **published by other accounts**.

- **Publish**: in *My library* mode, the **Publish** button on a personal item
  makes it visible to everyone. **Published ✓** removes it from sharing.
- **Import**: in *Community* mode, **Import** creates a **private copy** of the
  entry in your library (distinct id, freely editable).
- **Report**: reports an inappropriate entry, with an optional reason. An entry
  reported by **3 distinct accounts** is **hidden automatically** from the
  community; it stays in its author's personal library.

## Operating modes

A library operating mode describes a sequence of **elementary actions** (EBIOS
RM phase, target expressed as a supporting-asset **label**, MITRE technique)
plus indicative ratings. When importing into Workshop 4, map each action to the
**real supporting asset** in your study.
