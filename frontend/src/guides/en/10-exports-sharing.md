# Exports and sharing

## PDF reports

Each validated workshop produces a dedicated **PDF report** (study **Reports**
menu, or the download button in the workshop):

- Workshop 1: identity, business values, supporting assets, feared events,
  baseline gap.
- Workshop 2: RO/TO pairs and relevance.
- Workshop 3: ecosystem radar, scenario tree, attack paths.
- Workshop 4: operating modes, actions per phase, MITRE techniques, likelihood.
- Workshop 5: treatment plan, risk grid, residual mapping.
- **Compliance annex**: ISO 27001 or NIS2 coverage table.

## Office exports

From the study **dashboard**:

- **Risk register (Excel)**: five-sheet workbook — Summary, Risk scenarios,
  Treatment plan, Ecosystem, Compliance. Current state (not frozen to a
  *snapshot*). Auto-filters, adjusted columns.
- **Summary (Word)**: editable `.docx` document — identity, key figures, risk
  register, treatment plan. Useful to embed the analysis in a larger report.

From the **portfolio**: multi-study Excel export.

## JSON export / import

- **Export a study** produces a complete JSON file (all workshops).
- **Import a study** recreates the study on another installation or another
  account from that file. The five workshops restart as drafts; internal keys
  are re-mapped.
- **Duplicate a study** does the same internally, without a file — handy to
  create a reusable template.

## Multiple languages

The **FR / EN** selector (**Settings** menu) translates the interface shell
(navigation, cross-view headers) and these guides. The detailed methodological
content of the workshops and the PDF reports stay in French for now.

## Sharing a study

Study **Members** menu: invite an account by e-mail and assign a role (Reader,
Editor, Owner). See the *Getting started* guide for role details and the audit
log.
